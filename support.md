## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Lists, scanning, pasting and recipe import all work signed out, and your lists stay on your device. An account is only needed for syncing across devices and sharing lists with other people.

### How do I add a recipe?

Three ways, all on the list screen:

- **Scan** — point the camera at a cookbook page or a printed recipe.
- **Paste** — paste a block of text, or a link to a recipe page. Copia reads the ingredients off the page.
- **Browse recipes** — search Copia's recipe catalogue and add one directly.

### Why is my list in that order?

Copia sorts your list by the route you walk rather than the order you typed things in — produce first, then the aisles, then the back of the store. It learns your store's layout as you check things off, so the order improves the more you shop.

### How do I share a list?

Open a list, tap the **···** menu in the top right, and choose **Share list**. They'll need an account to join. Anything either of you checks off updates for both.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Copia. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and choose Copia. You can also reach it from Copia's own Settings screen.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the period you've already paid for runs out.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall in Copia and tap **Restore**. This re-checks your Apple Account for anything you've already bought, which is what you want after reinstalling or moving to a new phone.

### How do I delete my account?

In Copia, go to **Settings** and choose to delete your account. This removes your account and the lists stored on our server. It cannot be undone.

### Something isn't working

Email [craunryan@gmail.com](mailto:craunryan@gmail.com) with what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
